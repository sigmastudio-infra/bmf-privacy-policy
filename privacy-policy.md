## English

This policy explains what information **Be My Friends** (Android and iOS, app ID `com.sigmastudio.bemyfriends`, "the Game") collects, why, and how you can control it.

### 1. Information we collect

| Category | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account | A guest account identifier created by Firebase Authentication. If you choose to link Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, the player identifier and display name provided by that platform | To create your game account, restore your progress on a new device |
| Game data | Game progress, in-game currencies and items, settings, your in-game nickname, chat messages you send, and records of the actions you take in the Game | To run the Game; your progress is stored on our servers |
| Purchases | If in-app purchases are offered: product, price, time and store receipt identifier (we never receive your card or payment details) | To deliver purchases and handle refunds and disputes |
| Device and connection | IP address, device model, operating system and version, app version, language, country, and request times | To connect you to the right regional server, keep the service secure, and fix problems |
| Usage analytics | Events such as stages started, cleared or failed, screens opened and shop views, with an app instance identifier (Firebase Analytics) | To understand how the Game is played and improve it |
| Crash data | Crash reports and error logs, with device state and your game account identifier (Firebase Crashlytics, and error logs the Game sends to our servers) | To find and fix bugs |
| Advertising measurement | App events (such as install, app launch, level reached and purchases) and, where allowed, your device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking) sent to Meta through the Facebook SDK | To measure our advertising campaigns |

We do not ask for your real name, email address or phone number, and we do not access your location (other than country derived from IP address), contacts, camera, microphone or files.

### 2. Where your data is stored

The Game connects you to a regional server based on your country:
- **Korea:** game server in Seoul, Korea (Google Cloud); game database in Singapore (Neon, hosted on Amazon Web Services).
- **Other countries:** game server and database in Oregon, United States (Google Cloud; Neon on Amazon Web Services).

Analytics, crash and advertising data is processed by the service providers below, mainly in the United States.

### 3. Service providers

| Provider | Service | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Google LLC | Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config; Google Cloud (servers, logs) | Account identifier, usage, crash, device and connection data |
| Neon, Inc. | Game database hosting | Game data |
| Meta Platforms, Inc. | Facebook SDK (app events, ad measurement) | App events, advertising identifier, device data |
| Unity Technologies | Game engine, content delivery | Device and connection data |
| Google Play / Apple | Store, Play Games / Game Center sign-in, purchases | As described in their own policies |

We do not sell your personal information.

### 4. How long we keep it

- **Account and game data:** while your account exists. After you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
- **Purchase records:** 5 years, as required by Korean e-commerce law.
- **Server logs** (IP address, request records): up to 3 years, for security and dispute handling.
- **Analytics, crash and advertising data:** according to the retention settings of each provider above.

### 5. Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account and data:** email sigmastudio@sigmastudio.co.kr with the player ID shown in the Game. We may ask you to confirm the request.
- **Access or correct your data:** contact us at the same address.
- **Advertising identifier:** you can reset or limit it in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
- Uninstalling the Game stops further collection from your device, but does not delete your account data on our servers.

### 6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

### 7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to servers and databases is limited to authorized staff.

### 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and notify you in the Game for material changes.

### 9. Contact

Privacy officer: Sigma Studio Inc. — sigmastudio@sigmastudio.co.kr

실명·이메일·전화번호를 요구하지 않으며, 위치(IP 로 추정한 국가 제외)·연락처·카메라·마이크·파일에 접근하지 않습니다.

### 2. 보관 위치 (국외 이전 포함)

게임은 이용자의 국가에 따라 지역 서버에 연결합니다.
- **한국:** 게임 서버는 대한민국 서울(Google Cloud), 게임 데이터베이스는 싱가포르(Neon, Amazon Web Services 위).
- **그 외 국가:** 게임 서버와 데이터베이스 모두 미국 오리건(Google Cloud, Neon·Amazon Web Services).

분석·비정상 종료·광고 측정 정보는 아래 수탁사가 주로 미국에서 처리합니다. 이전은 게임 이용 중 네트워크로 수시로 이루어집니다.

### 4. 보유 기간과 파기

- **계정·게임 정보:** 계정이 있는 동안. 삭제를 요청하면 30일 안에 파기합니다.
- **결제 기록:** 전자상거래법에 따라 5년.
- **서버 로그**(IP 주소, 요청 기록): 보안·분쟁 대응을 위해 최대 3년.
- **분석·비정상 종료·광고 측정 정보:** 위 각 업체의 보관 설정에 따름.

전자적 파일은 복구할 수 없는 방법으로 삭제합니다.

### 5. 이용자의 권리

- **계정·데이터 삭제:** 게임에 표시된 플레이어 ID 와 함께 sigmastudio@sigmastudio.co.kr 로 요청해 주세요. 본인 확인을 요청할 수 있습니다.
- **열람·정정·처리 정지:** 같은 주소로 요청할 수 있습니다.
- **광고 식별자:** 기기 설정에서 재설정하거나 제한할 수 있습니다 (Android: 설정 → Google → 광고, iOS: 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적).
- 게임을 삭제하면 기기에서의 추가 수집은 멈추지만, 서버의 계정 정보는 삭제되지 않습니다.
